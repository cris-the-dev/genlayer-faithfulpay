Planned water outage on Tuesday

Water will be shut off on Tuesday, 3 November, from 9:00 to 14:00 in the Hill Street and Mill Lane area while a main valve is replaced.

Drinking water is available at the Hill Street depot during the outage. Bring your own container; each household may take up to 20 litres.

When the water comes back it may look cloudy. Run the cold tap for five minutes before drinking it.
